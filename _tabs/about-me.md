## Hello! I’m Sri Dhurkesh Sivaprakasam 👋
A passionate **Machine Learning Engineer** 🤖 with a love for solving real-world problems 🌍, bringing nearly 3 years of experience at Optisol Business Solutions.

**I’ve been thrilled to witness a groundbreaking shift in AI technology at an early stage of my career. Starting with CNN-based models that powered computer vision 🖼️, we’ve now seen the rise of transformer-based language models 🔗🧠, which are changing not just industries but the entire world.
It’s amazing to see how industries are finding new and creative ways to use AI in their work. This shift has sparked a wave of entrepreneurship 🚀, empowered people 🌟, and brought incredible changes to industries 🌍. Being part of this exciting time feels like witnessing history in the making!**

I’ve recently started blogging ✍️ to share knowledge and insights on topics that are either challenging to grasp 🤔 or have fascinated me 💡. My aim is to make these concepts accessible and showcase how technology can be leveraged to solve meaningful problems 💻✨. I'm also open to collaboration if anyone needs assistance with AI-related challenges.
